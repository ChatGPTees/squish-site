Squish

A watercolor paint box for iPhone Duo.

The bottom half of the screen is the paint box you had as a kid: eight watercolor cakes, a water cup and two mixing wells. The top half is the paper. Close the phone and your painting is saved. On other iPhones, tap the save button instead.

Support

How do I start? Tap the water cup to wet the brush, rub a paint cake until it shines, then paint on the paper.

Why is my yellow green? The brush still had blue on it. Rinse in the cup between colors, or don't, and keep the smear like everyone's paint box did.

Where are my paintings? Tap the paintings button. They are stored only on your iPhone.

Questions, problems or ideas: jason@chatgptees.co

Privacy policy

Squish does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. Your paintings are saved only on your device and are never sent anywhere. If you delete the app, your paintings are deleted with it.
